Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: July 2026

You can request deletion of personal data associated with your NHY-QR Ad Manager or NauticalReels account by following the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to admin@nautical.co.in from the email address associated with your account, or include the phone number used for OTP login.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include enough information for us to verify the account, such as your phone number, account name, or connected platform username.

What we delete

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize account data that is no longer required, including profile information, connected platform account records, OAuth tokens, campaign data, generated content, uploaded assets, scheduled posts, settings, and related operational records where applicable.

What may be retained

We may retain limited information when required for legal, tax, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, payment, or compliance reasons. Retained data will be limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

Connected platform access

You can also revoke this app’s access directly from the settings of Meta/Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, or other connected platforms. This stops future access, but you should still submit a deletion request if you want data already stored in the Service to be deleted.

Response time

We aim to respond to deletion requests within a reasonable period after verification. Complex requests, legal obligations, or security checks may require additional time.

Contact

For deletion questions, contact us at admin@nautical.co.in.
