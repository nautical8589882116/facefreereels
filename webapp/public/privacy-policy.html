Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NHY-QR Ad Manager and NauticalReels (“we”, “our”, or “the Service”) collect, use, and protect information when you use our dashboard, campaign tools, reel generation tools, and social publishing integrations.

Information we collect

  • Account information, such as your phone number, name, email address, and authentication status.
  • Verification information used for OTP-based login.
  • Campaign, ad copy, reel, asset, scheduling, and settings data that you create or upload.
  • Connected platform information, such as Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube account identifiers, page/channel names, profile URLs, follower counts, access tokens, refresh tokens, token expiry dates, and granted permissions.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To generate, store, schedule, and publish marketing content at your request.
  • To connect to third-party platforms such as Meta/Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.
  • To process subscriptions, payments, and billing support.
  • To troubleshoot, prevent abuse, improve reliability, and maintain security.
  • To communicate important service, security, or account notices.

Third-party services

The Service may use trusted third-party providers for hosting, database/storage, OTP delivery, payments, artificial intelligence generation, and social platform integrations. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to us or to you. Examples include Meta/Facebook, Instagram, Google/YouTube, Razorpay, Supabase, SMS/OTP providers, and AI model providers.

Social platform data

When you connect a Meta, Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube account, we request only the permissions needed to display connected account details and publish or manage content that you create through the Service. You can revoke access from the platform’s settings or disconnect the account inside the Service when available.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers, connected platforms, legal authorities when required by law, or other parties with your consent.

Your choices
